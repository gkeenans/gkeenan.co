Exploring Gdańsk, Katy suggested we make a habit out of buying a bag from a local roaster in every city we travel to, and I thought that was a lovely idea.

These are the beans that first inspired me to start tracking more closely. I was convinced I wanted to build an app for myself to keep a private little diary, where I could parse my favorite coffees and learn which regions and types and roast levels I was most drawn toward. I don't think I have the patience or knowledge do anything quite that complicated on this site, but at the very least I can document these things for myself and anyone else who might be interested.

Anyway, needless to say, this coffee is incredible. I think it's the first one that both Katy and I agreed is one of the best we've ever had. Triumph!

### Optimal cup
Honestly, didn't change a single thing the entire time I was brewing with them. I was so pleased with the result from the get go.

**Grind size:** 16
**Water temp:** 95 C
**Drawdown time:** ~3 minutes, pretty consistently
