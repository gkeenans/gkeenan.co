Ventured here because of the [Warsaw guide to coffee](https://sprudge.com/the-sprudge-guide-to-coffee-in-warsaw-poland-1133769.html) on Sprudge. Traveling alone with Olive while Katy is away. Space is small, quaint, and very dog friendly. Multiple other dogs passed through while I enjoyed my time.

Had an espresso shot pulled from Costa Rican beans. Incredible. Life-altering. Didn't realize espresso could taste like this. Reminded me of drinking sherry. Fruity, rich, dark chocolate. A lingering, silky texture. Astounding.

Followed it up with a cappuccino and a slice of cheesecake. Could have died happy right then and there.

Staff were incredibly friendly and knowledgable. Had a great conversation with one barista, Piotr, who convinced me[^1] to pick up a jar of the La Curva beans from Heresy, a local roastery where he works when he's not at the cafe. We also talked about coffee and Chicago and culture and cafes. Lots of ground covered in a short amount of time.

A special place. Immediately one of my favorite cafes I've been to. Can't wait to return.

[^1]: Not that I needed a ton of convincing, obviously.
